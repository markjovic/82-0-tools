82-0 tools

An overlay for 82-0.com that records each game you play, so the game's hidden scoring can be worked out and turned into pick advice.

Overlay bookmarklet. Save it as a bookmark’s URL, then run it on 82-0.com before your first pick. It only reads what the game already shows you and sends nothing to the game.

Drag to bookmarks bar

Where it’s up to. 82-0 works out your record on its own server, so the formula isn’t visible. The overlay logs every finished game in your browser; after about 30 games, export the log and the scoring can be fitted from it.
